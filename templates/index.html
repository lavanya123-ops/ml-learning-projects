About Strokewise AI

Strokewise AI was created to empower individuals with a deeper understanding of their health and potential stroke risk. Our platform uses advanced machine learning models trained on a comprehensive dataset of health metrics and lifestyle factors to provide a personalized risk assessment.

  • Our Methodology

    We use a highly accurate predictive model based on the Random Forest algorithm to analyze key health indicators.

  • Inform, Not Diagnose

    This tool is for informational purposes only. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

  • Driving Proactive Care

    Our mission is to help people identify potential risks early and encourage them to have informed conversations with healthcare professionals.
